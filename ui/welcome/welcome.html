Turn on protection?

Aether can block ads, trackers and harmful sites for you.

  • Pages load faster and use less data.
  • Fewer companies follow you from site to site.
  • Uses uBlock Origin, a free, open-source blocker trusted by millions.

Save data with Sip?

Sip is Aether's data saver, made for capped internet.

  • Shows how much data you use today, this month, and on which sites.
  • Keeps you inside a monthly budget, with a heads-up at 75%, 90% and 100%.
  • Stops videos from playing and loading by themselves.
  • Everything stays on your computer.

You're all set

A few things worth knowing:

  • Shift + click a link to Peek at it without leaving the page.
  • Ctrl + Alt + S puts two tabs side by side.
  • Ctrl + Alt + C hides the sidebar until you need it.
  • Workspaces sit under the address bar: + makes a new one.
  • Right-click the sidebar and choose Customize Aether… to change any of this later.
